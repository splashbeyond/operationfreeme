# TapJail App Store Resubmission

Apple review: June 9, 2026
Submission: 01d752ec-bf2f-4284-bfac-0aefd0eab7b0
Version: 1.0 (3)

## Required App Store Connect Changes

### 1. Remove Promotional Images

TapJail does not currently need promoted In-App Purchases on its App Store
product page.

In App Store Connect:

1. Open TapJail.
2. Open Monetization, then Subscriptions.
3. Open every subscription product, including Weekly and Yearly.
4. Remove the Promotional Image from each product.
5. Check any win-back offers and remove their promotional images or disable the
   offers if they are not being used.
6. Confirm no subscription uses the TapJail app icon as promotional artwork.

If promoted subscriptions are enabled later, each product needs its own unique
1024 x 1024 RGB JPG or PNG that represents that specific plan. Promotional
images cannot be the app icon or duplicates of another product's image.

### 2. Add the EULA to the App Description

Append these lines to the public App Description:

Terms of Use (EULA):
https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Privacy Policy:
https://tapjail.vercel.app/privacy.html

Use Apple's standard EULA for this submission. A custom EULA is not required.
The Privacy Policy URL should also remain in the dedicated Privacy Policy field.

## Corrected App Description

You said one minute. It's been an hour.

TapJail locks the apps you can't stop opening. The only way out is 100 taps.
Every time you break out, the price goes up.

Set a daily app limit for the apps that eat your time. When the limit runs out,
TapJail locks them. To get back in, open TapJail and tap the red circle 100
times.

No bypass. No grace period. No shortcut.

Most of the time, 100 taps will change your mind. You will put the phone down.
That is the design working.

When it is actually worth it, complete the taps to get 15 more minutes.

THE ESCALATING TAP COST

Every time you break out, the next breakout costs more.

First time: 100 taps - 15 minutes
Second time: 200 taps - 15 minutes
Third time: 400 taps - 15 minutes
Fourth time: 800 taps - 15 minutes
Every time after that: 1,000 taps - 15 minutes

Your app limit and tap count reset at midnight. Every day starts clean.

HOW IT WORKS

Authorize Screen Time. Pick the apps TapJail blocks. Set your daily app limit.
When the limit is reached, the apps lock. Want back in? Open TapJail and tap
your way out.

FEATURES

- Block apps, categories, and web domains
- Set one shared daily limit across selected apps
- Escalating tap cost: 100, 200, 400, 800, then 1,000
- Get 15 minutes of access after each completed breakout
- See the current tap requirement on the block screen
- Keep Screen Time data private and on your device
- No TapJail account or cloud sync

TapJail uses Apple's Screen Time APIs. Your app usage data stays on your device.
TapJail does not receive, store, or share that Screen Time data.
